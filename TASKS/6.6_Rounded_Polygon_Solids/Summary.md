# Summary of `T.rps` Rounded Polygon Solids

Parts and assemblies are now built from planar outlines.  New `scraftcam/solid.py` holds a `Solid` base class with `SolidStep` (one `.step` file), `SolidRoundedPolygon` (a tree of `Layer`s, each an immutable planar `RoundedPolygon` extruded from its parent's `z` to its own, adding or cutting, and each may be turned), `SolidRevolved` (a half profile turned about Z, tip at the origin, for round tools) and `SolidAssembly` (solids placed by position and an `(x, y, z, w)` quaternion, nested in SolidCraft's tree, one component per solid and one color per solid).  `gui/outline_polygon.py` makes polygons and revolved solids from outline files, so one file gives both the diagram and the solid.  First cases: Shars 202-1664's jaw layout, checked live against `JawPlate`; the Kurt DX6 vise (`tooling/kurt_vise.py`, three parts that are its three keep-out zones, the movable jaw placed by the gap); a setup with two parallels (`tooling/vise_setup.py`); the example drill revolved.  `Parallel` moved onto the new solids and `Profile` was removed.  No optional units: the old `geometry.RoundedPolygon` stays unitless and its messages are marked with the unit.  Left elsewhere: keep-outs and moving `keepout.py` and `demo.py` (`T.kz~4`), the editor's Mirror flag (`T.oe~6.2`), holes (`T.sfoe~6.4` #290), the tooling plate's grid of holes.
