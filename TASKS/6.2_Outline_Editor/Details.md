# 6.2 Outline Editor

(Draft by Claude, 2026-11-17, from the user's #43; widened
2026-11-22 to take in `T.sfoe~6.4`, its SVG.)

A GUI in which a user makes a parametric 2D outline without writing
Python, for their own tools and parts, and the drawing of it: one
outline file (letters table plus sketch) gives the diagram (`.svg`)
for panels and documents, and the solid (`T.tfo~6.5`).

* The editor: corners as formulas in letters, fillets, marks,
  dimensions, hide strips, holes, [Revolve] for round tools, errors
  in the user's own units, light and dark.
* The SVG: the same meaning as the screen, without the editing aids,
  drawn by the same code; black on white, written beside the outline.
* Features are added as the tools of `T.tfo~6.5` need them.
